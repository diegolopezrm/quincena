Privacy and personal data policy

In force since October 3, 2026. This is a translation; the Spanish version governs.

3. What it is used for

  • Showing your accounts, movements, balances and projections, and recording what you capture or import.
  • Answering the questions you ask Gemini.
  • Reviewing the Gemini answers you report, to correct them and filter the ones that follow.
  • Checking that requests to Gemini and reports come from the app, and limiting abuse.
  • Reading prices, rates and balances from the sources you connect.

Quincena does not use your data for advertising, commercial profiles or analytics, and does not sell it.

4. Your rights

You have the right to know, update and correct your data; to ask for proof of your authorization; to be told how it is used; to revoke the authorization and ask for it to be deleted; to access it free of charge; and to file complaints with Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.

Since your financial data is on your device, you can exercise most of these rights in the app itself: see and correct any movement, export everything to a file, turn off each optional feature, disconnect Binance and delete everything from Settings. For anything else, write to admin@dlsoft.dev. Questions are answered within ten business days and claims within fifteen, as the law requires.

5. Authorization

By using Quincena you authorize the handling described here. Each optional feature asks for your permission separately before it turns on: notifications, location, Gemini, Binance, wallets and syncing between your devices. You can turn it off at any time. A report about an answer leaves only when you send it.

6. Minors and sensitive data

Quincena is not directed at people under 18 and does not ask for sensitive data.

7. International transfers

Some of the providers above process data outside Colombia, mainly in the United States. They only receive what the table in section 2 shows, when you use the feature that needs them.

8. Security and retention

The app's data lives on your device and is protected by its own system; the Binance key, the sync code and the backup code are kept in the keychain; every connection, every sync file and every backup, unless you choose the unencrypted file, is encrypted. Data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. The anonymous Firebase account is deleted after 30 days, and reports about answers after 90.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this same page with the date it takes effect.
