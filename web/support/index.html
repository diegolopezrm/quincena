Where is my data?

On your device. Quincena has no server holding your accounts or movements. If you change phones, export your data from Settings and import it on the new one.

How do payments get recorded on their own?

In Settings, Automatic capture. On iPhone with iOS 27 there are ready shortcuts for your banks' notifications, texts, Apple Pay and screenshots; on Android, the app reads the banks' notifications if you allow it.

Is it safe to connect Binance?

Quincena only takes a read-only key: it cannot buy, sell, move or withdraw anything. The key is kept in your device's keychain and requests go straight to Binance.

Does Quincena give financial or investment advice?

No. It shows your figures and helps you decide, but it does not recommend buying or selling anything.
